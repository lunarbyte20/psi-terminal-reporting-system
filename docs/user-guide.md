# PSI Terminal Reporting — User Guide

## 1. Signing In & First-Time Setup

- Sign in with the email and temporary password provided by the Admin.
- On your first sign-in, you will be prompted to set a permanent password.
- You can manage your profile, password, two-factor authentication, or passkeys under **Settings**.

## 2. Supervisors

1. Open **My Weekly Reports** from the sidebar.
2. Select the current reporting week and click **Encode report** (or **Edit** on a Draft / Returned report).
3. Enter the nine peso sales categories and ridership trips per vehicle type. Passengers per trip are pre-filled from your terminal's previous report.
4. Your edits are automatically backed up locally in your browser; click **Save draft** to save on the server, or **Submit for review** when ready.
5. If the Admin returns a report, read the return note at the top of the edit page, correct the figures, and submit again.
6. Record terminal issues and action items under **Issues & Actions** (or directly in the terminal issues section of a report).

## 3. Admin (Sarah)

- **Review & Bulk Approve**: Review submitted reports individually, or use **Approve all** on the Weekly Reports board to approve all waiting reports for the week (or for a selected supervisor tab).
- **Save and Approve**: When encoding a report directly, use **Save and approve** to publish it in one step.
- **Reminders**: Click **Send reminders** on Weekly Reports to send an in-app reminder (bell icon in the header; no email) to supervisors who still have not-started, draft, or returned terminals for the week.
- **Evaluation & Recommendations**: Write the weekly key concerns, overall evaluation, numbered recommendations, and any applicable detailed operational/departmental sections under **Evaluation & Recommendations**.
- **Administration**: Manage user accounts, terminals, supervisors, vehicle types, reporting weeks, and review the **Audit Log**.

## 4. Management (CEO, General Manager, COO)

- **Executive Dashboard**: View approved sales, variance, ridership, supervisor rankings, and terminal breakdowns by **Week** or **Month**.
- **Exports & Printing**: Download the terminal breakdown and ridership summary as **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV**, or open **Management report (PDF)** to print or save an 8.5 × 13 in PDF report. The PDF and the Excel **Management Summary** sheet both include the Top 3 highest/lowest sales and Top 3 increase/decrease lists, open issues (without tags), the evaluation as a *Management Matter | Evaluation / Recommendation* table, the issue **Status Summary**, and the signature block. Every printed page has a 0.45–0.5 in margin, including pages after the first.
- **Report signatories** (Admin): *Administration → Report signatories* sets the "Prepared by" and "Submitted to" names and titles printed at the end of every report. Until saved, the names from the PSI summary workbook are used.
- **Evaluation, History & Ridership**: Read weekly evaluations, browse past reports, and review monthly ridership consolidations.
